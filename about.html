About

Speaking is a powerful tool for discovery, reflection, and self-expression.

The Goal

Jericho Open Mic hopes to cultivate an encouraging community of writers, artists, photographers, and more who support each other's work and the bravery it takes to speak up about something they feel strongly about. Whether gracing our website through recordings of recitations or joining us at local libraries and cafés, Jericho Open Mic has a place for you.
